## Key Patterns Across Regions

### Growth vs. Distribution Trade-off:
- **Post-Soviet**: Lower growth but better distribution (poverty/inequality reduction)
- **Latin America**: Higher growth but worse distribution (poverty/inequality increase)
- **Western Europe & N. America**: Moderate effects on both dimensions

### Policy Implications:
1. **Post-Soviet**: Policies reduce poverty/inequality but at cost of growth
2. **Latin America**: Policies boost growth but increase poverty/inequality
3. **Overall**: Context matters - effects vary dramatically by region
