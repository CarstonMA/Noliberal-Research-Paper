\section{Event-study estimates by outcome and policy component}
\label{sec:appendix-event-studies}

This appendix reports dynamic event-study estimates following Callaway and Sant'Anna, estimated separately for each outcome and each liberalization component (Fraser and Heritage sub-indices). Event time is years relative to the first period a country is classified as treated for that component, as defined in the main text. Each point shows the aggregated average treatment effect at that event time with its confidence interval; \emph{no} event-time period is omitted or normalized to zero for identification---unlike a standard two-way fixed-effects event study with a single dropped pre-period, the last pre-treatment year ($e=-1$) is estimated like the other leads and lags whenever the procedure identifies it.
